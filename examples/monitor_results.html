How the decisions are made
Acceptability probability (pi_final, pi_min)
Each dimension is cut into consecutive subgroups of 5 in the recorded order. After every subgroup, an exponentially discounted Normal-Inverse-Gamma posterior of the process mean and standard deviation (forgetting factor 0.95) gives pi_t = P(q >= q0 | data so far), where q = Phi((USL - mu)/sigma) - Phi((LSL - mu)/sigma) is the conformance probability and q0 = 0.997; pi_t is estimated from 2000 posterior draws. pi_final is pi at the last subgroup, pi_min the lowest pi from subgroup t_min = 10 on. The default (tolerance-aware) prior is centred at the nominal with scale (USL - LSL)/8 and carries almost no weight.
Zones (zone, first_intervention)
continue when pi >= 0.95; intervene when pi <= 0.8 and t >= 10; monitor otherwise. zone is the zone of the last subgroup, first_intervention the first subgroup in the intervene zone.
Category (category)
persistent low-confidence: pi_final <= 0.8; recovered transient: pi_min <= 0.8 and pi_final >= 0.95; no intervention-zone entry: pi_min > 0.8; borderline final: the rest. Assigned in this order.
Ppk_normal
Empirical capability from all values with the overall standard deviation, for comparison with pi_final; it plays no part in the monitoring.
Read with care
q0, the thresholds, the forgetting factor, the prior, the subgroup size and t_min are engineering and operational policy parameters, not universal constants; these defaults are the validation settings of the article. The Gaussian model is a working approximation: skewed or contaminated data, serial correlation and gauge error change how often the rule acts. A first intervention at t_min usually means pi was already low at the start of the record, not that a change began there. The zones support the engineer's decision after approval; they do not replace control charts or the approval itself.
