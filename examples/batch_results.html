How the decisions are made
Threshold rule (threshold_decision)
ACCEPT when the capability estimate is at least C0 = 1.33. It ignores how uncertain the estimate is.
Risk-calibrated rule (decision)
ACCEPT when the probability that the true capability is below C0 (p_fail) is at most alpha = 0.05 (from the cost ratio 19: alpha = 1/(1 + ratio)). For normal data p_fail = Phi((C0 - estimate) / SE) with SE = sqrt((1/9 + estimate^2/2) / n), which is the same as: estimate at least required = C0 + k x SE, k = z(1 - alpha) = 1.6449. For non-normal data (percentile method) p_fail comes from a nonparametric bootstrap (resample, refit); required is then shown for reference.
Reversed (reversed)
The two rules disagree. With alpha below 0.5 this happens when the estimate is at least C0 but not far enough above it for its uncertainty.
Read with care
These rules support decisions under limited samples; they do not replace the capability requirement itself. The UC-Cap bands and signal rules are illustrative, not general standards, and the score orders the review: the engineer confirms the process condition and chooses the action. Each dimension's report gives the numbers behind its decision.
